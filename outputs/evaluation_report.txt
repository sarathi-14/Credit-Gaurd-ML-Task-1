3. BUSINESS & FINANCIAL RISK TRADEOFF ANALYSIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
In credit risk modeling, False Negatives (predicting a defaulting borrower as safe)
carry significantly higher financial costs than False Positives (flagging a safe borrower
for secondary review). Models trained with balanced class weights optimize the recall
and area under the ROC curve, ensuring high-risk profiles are effectively identified.
